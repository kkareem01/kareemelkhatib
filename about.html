Background

My Story

Electrical engineering, AI, and a family business — how the three fit together.

Who is Kareem Elkhatib?

I’m an electrical engineering student at the University of Georgia, class of 2028, based in Atlanta. I grew up around our family’s menswear business, and today I split my time between the physics underneath modern hardware and building AI-powered software for the engineering and business worlds.

01 — Mission

Make AI do real work

Not demos, not hype — agents, automations, and tools that handle actual work end-to-end for the electrical engineering and business industries. Hardware or software, my habit is the same: understand how the thing works, then build it properly.

02 — Roots

Where I’m from

Georgia. Our family runs Suit Station, a menswear business, and that’s where I learned business hands-on — running sales campaigns and funnels, and building the inventory and reorder systems behind them. Now I’m in Atlanta studying electrical engineering at UGA: circuits, signals, and proving things on a bench instead of just on paper.

03 — The Spark

How I found AI

Through real problems. Working sales — hundreds of calls, follow-ups, and tracking — showed me how much of business is repeatable process. Engineering showed me that anything understood well enough can be built better: like the external camera power system I designed for my own film rigs, which took runtime up roughly twenty-fold. AI is where those two lessons meet, so I started building agents and automations — including the CRM I built and run for my own work.

04 — Results

Results so far

Closed $20,000 in revenue as a sales manager running a high-ticket framework, and lifted show rates 12% across 800 calls. Generated 40 qualified leads and $6,000 in revenue in one month for the family business. Engineered a camera power system that turned minutes of runtime into full shoot days — validated under load, in the field.

05 — Now

What I’m working on

Building AI-powered software and systems for the electrical engineering and business industries, while finishing my BS in electrical engineering at UGA (May 2028). Off the clock, I’m behind a camera or on a mic — filming around Atlanta and public speaking at UGA.
