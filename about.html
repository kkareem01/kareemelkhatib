Background

My Story

Electrical engineering, digital marketing, and a camera — how the three fit together.

Who is Kareem Elkhatib?

I’m an electrical engineering student at the University of Georgia and a digital builder focused on AI automation, marketing, and visual storytelling. Most days I split my time three ways: building digital marketing systems for service-based businesses, helping run my family’s suit business, and studying electrical engineering at UGA.

The rest of my time goes to a camera or a stage. Photography, videography, and public speaking have always been part of how I create.

01

What I build

I help service businesses that are great at what they do but almost invisible online. I build the websites, Google presence, and marketing systems that get them found, get them contacted, and get them more customers.

My process starts with diagnosis, not a pitch. I figure out what is actually holding the business back first — and if I don’t think I can help, I don’t sell anything.

02

How I think about marketing

People are more skeptical of marketing than ever. I don’t think the answer is better tricks. It’s being clearer, more useful, and more honest about what you can actually do for someone. That’s the standard I hold my own work to.

03

Where I started

I’m 21, based between Athens and Atlanta. I grew up in Gwinnett County, around Norcross.

My first job was at Target. I mostly learned how to fold clothes, but it was my first look at how a large company actually operates. The real education was my family’s suit business, where I got interested in sales, marketing, and how customers decide where to spend their money.

04

Finding AI

Discovering AI agents changed what I thought one person could build. I started with websites, then began helping local businesses improve how they showed up online — which pulled me past web design and into Google marketing, SEO, conversion optimization, and automation.

I didn’t want to make another AI-generated website. I wanted to understand what makes someone find a business, trust it, contact it, and become a customer.

05

Selected work

Websites and digital systems for my own businesses and for local service businesses:

  • SuitStation.us — men’s retail
  • byKareem.Elkhatib.co — photography
  • GoldenStateRehab.com — rehabilitation
  • ChelseaManorRecovery.com — rehabilitation
  • ConserveHeatingAndCooling.com — home services

06

What I’m working toward

Right now, I’m learning how to build a great digital marketing agency for service-based businesses. Long term, I want to build a client acquisition agency specifically for engineering firms — the place where my engineering and business backgrounds overlap.

There’s a bigger goal behind that. I don’t believe everyone needs to do what they love. I think there’s more value in finding what you’re unusually good at, becoming excellent at it, and using that to create opportunities for yourself and others. I’m still figuring that out for myself — shiny object syndrome makes it easy to jump between opportunities instead of staying long enough to become great. If I can beat that, build something meaningful, and eventually help other people make better decisions about what to study or pursue, that’s a career worth having.
